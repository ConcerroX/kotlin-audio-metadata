Id3v2Tag

class Id3v2Tag : Id3Tag

A parsed ID3v2 tag.

Frames are exposed as they were read, including the ones without a typed mapping. Typed properties resolve the common frames listed in the ID3v2.3/2.4 specifications.

The tag is mutable and not thread-safe. Id3v2Frame.content is a read-only view.
